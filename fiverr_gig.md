## Gig description (ô Description)

**Turn your foreign-language video into a clean Vietnamese version — fast.**

I translate the speech in your video, generate a natural Vietnamese voiceover, and deliver a finished video with subtitles built in. Great for:
- Creators localizing content for Vietnamese audiences
- Film/anime recap & review channels
- Tutorials, courses, product & marketing videos

**What makes my dubs clean:**
- **Context-aware translation** (not word-by-word) — names and terms stay consistent
- **Sentence-level timing** — the voiceover stays in sync with the original; no rushing or overlap
- **Original background music & sound kept** — I separate the voice from the music, so only the speech is replaced (no dead, silent video)
- **Subtitles included** — burned into the video, plus an editable `.srt` file on Standard/Premium
- **Works on long videos** — even hours of footage

**Honest note:** this is professional **voiceover-style dubbing** (narration over the video), not lip-synced dubbing. I use a high-quality AI voice and review every delivery for accuracy.

I built the tool behind this service myself — open source: **github.com/kienggggg/auto-video-dub**

Send me your video and target language, and I'll get started. Message me first if your video is longer than 45 minutes or needs a custom quote.

## FAQ (thêm 3-4 câu)
**Q: Which languages do you support?**
A: I specialize in Vietnamese voiceover (my native language, so I can guarantee quality). Subtitles are available in many languages — message me for other voice languages.

**Q: Is this an AI voice or a human voice?**
A: A high-quality AI voice, tuned for a natural narration style. I review timing and translation on every order so it sounds clean, not robotic.

**Q: Will the background music be removed?**
A: No — on Standard and Premium I keep the original music and sound effects and place the new voice on top (only the original speech is replaced).

**Q: My video is 3 hours long. Can you do it?**
A: Yes. My tool handles long videos. Message me for a custom quote before ordering.
